# D245 — Plain `claude` output is returned unstripped

## Context

Seeds 16 and 18 of `llm-endpoint` each failed only `AC-ENDPOINT-012`'s
plain-text case, in opposite directions: one returned `"hello\n"` where
the criterion then said `"hello"`, the other stripped it after the
criterion was corrected to `"hello\n"`. `R-ENDPOINT-003`'s "the reply as
printed" read both ways.

## Decision

`R-ENDPOINT-003` says output that is not the stream is the reply
unchanged, never stripped.

## Consequences

`llm-endpoint` is regenerated.
