# D282 — `verifier-orchestrator`'s bundle carries `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md`

## Context

Seed 9 of `verifier-orchestrator` measures mutation correctly in the
real repo and failed only `AC-VERIFY-005`, whose test checks that its
fixture lints clean. The fixture's criterion had no Given step, which
`R-LINT-010` fails though the step grammar allows it; the lint rules
are in `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md`, which the bundle did not include.

## Decision

`interface/library.md`'s `Implements:` line adds `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md`,
as `D265` did for `impact-analyzer`.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated.
