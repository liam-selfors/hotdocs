# D241 — Glossary entries, guarantee headings, the `D1`–`D14` exemption and CLI findings are exact

## Context

Seed 13 of `spec-linter`, the first to reproduce the hand-written
linter's report on the whole corpus, passed 30 of 35 criteria at mutation
1.0. Four of the five failures were rules the hand-written linter applied
and no text stated: a glossary entry is a `- **Term**` list item (a test
wrote the bold without the bullet); a guarantee's heading must equal its
filename (`R-LINT-017` said "matching its own filename" of the id);
`R-LINT-030` exempts `D1`–`D14`, frozen at the baseline reset and never
files (the criterion mentioned them, the requirement did not); and
`R-LINT-031` names the component by id. The fifth, a size finding the
candidate missed, was its own.

## Decision

`SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §3 defines a glossary entry's form. `R-LINT-017`,
`R-LINT-030` and `R-LINT-031` state the rest, in no more words than
before.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated.
