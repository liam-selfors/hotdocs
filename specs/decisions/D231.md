# D231 — `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` defines per-file and `Purpose` word counts and where `R-LINT-031` reports

## Context

Seed 8 of `spec-linter`, comparing its report on the whole corpus with
the hand-written linter's, differed in two places. It counted per-file
words its own way, so twenty files moved across a threshold and one,
`R-GQE-002` (SQL included), across the hard cap, which stopped
verification. And it reported `R-LINT-031` on `component.md` where the
hand-written linter used `interface/cli.md`. `D211` had defined the
eight size dimensions but not these counts, and nothing said where a
CLI-coverage finding goes.

## Decision

§4 defines both word counts as `str.split()` tokens, exactly what the
hand-written linter computed, and §5 says the `R-LINT-031` finding is on
`interface/cli.md`, line 1. `spec-linter` has no words to spare under its
cap, and these rules already belong to the guidelines it implements.

## Consequences

A regenerated linter reproduces the hand-written linter's report.
