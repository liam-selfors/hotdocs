# D73 — builds_on: a decision may depend on prior reasoning without replacing it

## Context

`supersedes` models exactly one relationship, full replacement. Many decisions depend on a prior one's reasoning without replacing any of it — informally, in prose only, with no structured fact a tool could walk.

## Decision

A new, optional, list-valued field, `builds_on` — the decisions a decision's own reasoning depends on. Many-valued, unlike `supersedes`, and carrying no status obligation on either side. Every entry must resolve to a real decision, the same mechanical check `supersedes` uses, minus the status-consistency half (which only makes sense for a replacement relationship).
