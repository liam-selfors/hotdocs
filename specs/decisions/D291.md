# D291 — `docs-site-renderer`'s `main` takes the tokens after `build`

## Context

`D285` said `docs_site_renderer.cli.main` receives the tokens from
`build` on, copying `corpus-graph-builder`'s wording, and `D289`'s
`R-DOCS-007` and `AC-DOCS-007` followed it. But `hotdocs-cli`'s verb
table strips both words of `docs-site build`, so seed 6, built to `D285`,
landed with every criterion passing and then answered
`hotdocs docs-site build --out=…` with a usage error.

## Decision

`interface/library.md`, `R-DOCS-007` and `AC-DOCS-007` say `main`
receives only the tokens after `build`, as `hotdocs-cli`'s verb table
already has it. The first-token check is dropped.

## Consequences

`docs-site-renderer` is regenerated; seed 6 was reverted before commit.
