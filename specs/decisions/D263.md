# D263 — `AC-DRIFT-006` covers the tool-root fallback

## Context

Seeds 2 and 4 of `decision-drift-detector` passed every criterion but
scored 0.73 and 0.76 on mutation. Most survivors were in `R-DRIFT-004`'s
fallback to the tool root and its package-shaped load, which no
criterion exercised.

## Decision

`AC-DRIFT-006` loads the component from a tool root with no `gen/`
under `repo_root`, against a package-shaped fake, as `AC-CORPUS-004`
does for `corpus-loader`.

## Consequences

`decision-drift-detector` is regenerated.
