# D21 — A dedicated Rationale section keeps Purpose dependent-safe, mechanically enforced

## Decision

`component.md` has an optional `Rationale` section, placed after `Purpose`. `Purpose` MUST describe what a unit does and why it exists, never how that design was arrived at — decision citations, historical justification, and cross-references to the discussion that produced the current design belong in `Rationale` instead, which a component may omit entirely if it needs no supporting history. `spec-linter` fails any `Purpose` section containing a decision citation.

## Consequences

`Purpose` is guaranteed dependent-safe by construction, extractable verbatim by anything building a compiled, outward-facing component summary.
