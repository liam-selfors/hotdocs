# D244 — `Corpus.md` says what `Component.root` holds and how quotes are read

## Context

Seed 3 of `corpus-loader` failed two criteria. It set `Component.root` to
the component's directory, where every consumer reads it as the
`component.md` `Doc`; the field was named but never defined. And it kept
the quotes around a quoted frontmatter value, which "an unquoted string"
left open.

## Decision

`Corpus.md` defines `root` and the other single-`Doc` fields, and says
surrounding quotes are removed, with an example.

## Consequences

`corpus-loader` is regenerated.
