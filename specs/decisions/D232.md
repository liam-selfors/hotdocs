# D232 — An acceptance file's `Covers:` tag is its first one

## Context

Seed 9 of `spec-linter` reported six hard failures in the real corpus,
all `R-LINT-009`: it read every `Covers:` in an acceptance file as a
reference, including the ones criteria quote in their own examples
(`Covers: R-FOO-003`, "a `Covers:` tag"). The hand-written linter and
`graph-query-engine`'s `edges` view take the first match only, but the
guidelines never said which `Covers:` is the tag.

## Decision

§5 says an acceptance file's tag is its first `Covers:`, later ones are
prose, and a capability scenario's is the line under its heading.

## Consequences

The regenerated linter reads covers the way the views already do.
