# D20 — llm-endpoint's model_call_error is a structured, categorized object, and it keeps its own call log

## Decision

`model_call_error` is `{category, message}` with `category` one of `network`/`auth`/`rate_limit`/`timeout` — never a boolean collapse that makes "how many failures were rate-limits vs. auth errors" unanswerable even in principle. `ModelResponse` carries an output-side `backend` field. `llm-endpoint` keeps its own call log, caller-agnostic and transport-level only, since it is the one chokepoint every model call passes through regardless of caller.
