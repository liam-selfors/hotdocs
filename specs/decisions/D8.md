# D8 — merge-orchestrator processes every impacted component to completion before evaluating block status

## Decision

Every component in a merge's blast radius MUST be attempted — generated and verified, or routed through its sealed-component path — before the overall block decision is evaluated. No fail-fast short-circuiting. This requirement constrains the outcome (every component attempted) rather than the mechanism (sequential vs. concurrent).
