# D264 — `AC-LINT-021` spells out its corpus and its only findings

## Context

Seeds 3 and 4 of `spec-linter` each failed `AC-LINT-021` on a test that
gave comp-bar a second requirement, left it uncovered by pointing
`AC-BAR-002` at a missing id, and then expected no finding for it. Both
candidates were right. The criterion never said which requirements
comp-bar has, and two test authors filled the gap the same wrong way.

## Decision

`AC-LINT-021` gives comp-bar exactly one requirement, covered by
`AC-BAR-001`, and names the only two `R-LINT-008`/`009` findings
expected.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated.
