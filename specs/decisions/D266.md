# D266 — Usage cards skip unreadable signatures; how bundle text is joined

## Context

Seed 1 of `spec-bundle-assembler` passed every criterion, but on the
real corpus it crashed building the usage card for `llm-endpoint`,
whose exception classes have no signature `inspect` can read, so every
dependent of `llm-endpoint` would fail to assemble. Its text fields also
joined documents differently from the version it replaced. Neither case
was specified.

## Decision

- `R-ASSEMBLE-011`: the card lists public top-level functions and
  classes the module defines, by sorted name, leaving out any whose
  signature `inspect` cannot read. Its closing rationale is shortened
  to keep the component under its word cap.
- `SpecBundle.md`: each text field joins stripped, non-empty document
  bodies with one blank line between.

## Consequences

`spec-bundle-assembler` is regenerated, and its bundles are compared
with the previous version's for every component before it lands.
