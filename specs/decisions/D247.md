# D247 — `AC-ENDPOINT-013` covers configuration and provider detection

## Context

Seeds 19 and 20 of `llm-endpoint` passed every criterion but reached
mutation scores of 0.78 and 0.73. Their surviving mutants sat in
`load_config`'s error paths, `save_config` (creating a directory,
writing and dropping `timeout_seconds`) and `detect_providers`, which
no criterion exercised.

## Decision

`AC-ENDPOINT-013` covers them, with every expected value checked against
the hand-written `llm-endpoint` first. To stay under the word cap,
`constraints.md` and `component.md`'s Out of scope are condensed to what
the requirements do not already say.

Seed 21 read "`None` when there is none" in `R-ENDPOINT-007` as "no
file", and raised for a file without an `[llm]` table; the requirement
now names both cases.

## Consequences

`llm-endpoint` is regenerated.
