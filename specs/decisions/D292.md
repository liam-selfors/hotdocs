# D292 — `provenance-tracker`'s usage errors print their reason

## Context

Seed 2 of `provenance-tracker` landed with every criterion passing, and
then `hotdocs verify` with no `--model`, where a capability needed a
fresh run, exited `2` and printed nothing. `interface/cli.md` listed the
exit codes but never said to print the cause, and under `D252` the
candidate added nothing unrequested. The previous version had printed
it.

## Decision

`interface/cli.md` says every usage error prints one line naming its
cause to stderr before exiting `2`. The change log points to the
decisions instead, keeping the component under its word cap.

## Consequences

`provenance-tracker` is regenerated, then the components whose bundles
hold its code: `review-packet-builder`, `provenance-tracker-ui` and
`component-gate-runner`.
