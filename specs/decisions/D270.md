# D270 — The generator's reply format moves to its interface

## Context

`D268` put the reply format in `R-GEN-015`. Seed 2 of `generator` then
failed `AC-GEN-006` and `AC-GEN-017`, whose tests mocked replies without
the nonce, since a criterion's test is written from its own requirement
and `interface/`, the problem `D269` fixed for `test-author`.

## Decision

The format moves from `R-GEN-015` to `interface/library.md`, which every
criterion's author sees; `R-GEN-015` points to it.

## Consequences

`generator` is regenerated.
