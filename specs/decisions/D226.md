# D226 — `criterion-compiler`'s criteria cover every literal form and type construction

## Context

Seed 11 of `criterion-compiler` passed every criterion and reached a
mutation score of 0.66. Its surviving mutants were nearly all in literal
parsing (numbers with a fraction or exponent, `true`/`false`/`null`,
nested arrays, an escaped quote) and in the `a <Type> with <field>: ...`
template's field check. No criterion exercised them, so the tests could
not tell a correct parser from a broken one.

## Decision

`AC-COMPILE-016` compiles one criterion per literal form and checks the
exact emitted value, and builds a declared type with declared and
undeclared fields. The `component.md` Open questions section, three
resolved items already recorded in requirements, is reduced to one line
to make room.

## Consequences

A generated compiler is now tested on the literal grammar the step
grammar defines.
