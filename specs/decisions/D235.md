# D235 — The step grammar says where a criterion's steps are

## Context

Seeds 15 and 16 of `criterion-compiler` failed nearly every criterion,
and seed 16's errors showed why: it read the ```` ```gherkin ```` fence
line as a step. The grammar defined each step's form but never said
which lines of an acceptance file are steps, how a wrapped step
continues, how scenarios separate, or what a line number counts. The
hand-written compiler settled all of it in code.

## Decision

`specs/architecture/step-phrase-grammar.md` gains "Where the steps are",
stating the hand-written compiler's rules: the first ```` ```gherkin ````
block, keyword lines start steps, other non-blank lines continue them, a
blank line ends one, a `Given` after a `Then` starts a scenario, and line
numbers count lines in the file.

## Consequences

`criterion-compiler` is regenerated.
