# D25 — `golden-scenario-verifier` extracted from `merge-orchestrator`; a general decomposition lesson added to `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §4

## Context

`D24`'s `R-MERGE-020` addition pushed `merge-orchestrator`'s
`spec_bundle_words` to 4074 — over D2's 4000 hard-fail cap, and the
exact outcome `pipeline-decomposition.md`'s own "finding" section had
already predicted after `D22`: fan-out (14, already double the warn
threshold) and word count crowding their ceilings *at the same time*,
not just either alone. The question: is that pairing a sign the caps
are set too low, or a sign of a genuinely over-complicated spec?

## Decision

**A real decomposition signal, not a calibration problem — with a
precedent already in the corpus.** The golden-scenario gate
(`R-MERGE-014`'s dispatch, `R-MERGE-020`'s capability-intersection
lookup) is, structurally, the cross-component analog of what
`verifier-orchestrator` already does per-component. It lived inside
`merge-orchestrator` only because `D16` added it to an existing
sequencing step, not because it conceptually belongs there. Extracted
into a new component, `golden-scenario-verifier`.
`merge-orchestrator`'s `R-MERGE-014` is now a thin call-out — *when*
and *with what* it gets called — not the dispatch mechanics themselves.

Fan-out stayed at 14 (`criterion-compiler` swapped for
`golden-scenario-verifier`) — extraction swaps one dependency for
another, it doesn't reduce call count. `spec_bundle_words` is what
actually moved, dropping well clear of both thresholds.

## Consequences

**General lesson, written into `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §4:** two
independent size-cap dimensions crowding their ceilings for the same
component at once is a stronger, different signal than either alone —
it means the component isn't just calling a lot of neighbors
(fan-out), it's also carrying enough decision logic to show up in word
count too. The fix is to look for that logic's own identity, starting
with whether a structural precedent for the split already exists
elsewhere in the corpus — not to raise the cap, and not to expect
fan-out itself to drop as proof the split worked.

See `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §4, `specs/architecture/
pipeline-decomposition.md`'s "Resolved (D25)" note, and
`specs/components/golden-scenario-verifier/` for the current,
authoritative rules.
