# D274 — `verifier-orchestrator`'s interface gives dependency paths and fixture guidance

## Context

Seed 1 of `verifier-orchestrator` failed eleven criteria, while its
candidate passed the whole hand suite. The generated tests planted a
fake `criterion-compiler` under `gen/`, though it is sealed and loaded
from `pipeline/`, and sixteen of them made the fixture component
`sealed`, as `test-author`'s prompt advises by default, so it ran only
its characterization tests (`R-VERIFY-017`). Neither fact was visible
to a criterion's test author.

## Decision

`interface/library.md` gives where each dependency is loaded from, and
says a fixture component under test is never `sealed` unless its
criterion concerns sealed verification. The change log points to the
decisions to keep the component under its word cap.

The hand suite's fixtures also copy `corpus-loader`, `citation-checker`
and `size-checker`, which `spec-linter` has loaded since `D242`.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated.
