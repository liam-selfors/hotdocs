# D239 — `criterion-compiler`'s output path and `InterfaceSurface` are exact

## Context

Seed 17 of `criterion-compiler` failed three criteria. With `out_dir`
outside `repo_root`, it crashed making the output path repo-relative;
the hand-written compiler fell back to the absolute path. A test built
`InterfaceSurface(module_name=...)` alone, where the candidate required
every field; the spec never gave its fields. The third was the
candidate's own line counting.

## Decision

`CompileReport.md` says `file` is relative to `repo_root`, or absolute
outside it. `interface/library.md` gives `InterfaceSurface`'s fields, all
defaulted.

## Consequences

`criterion-compiler` is regenerated.
