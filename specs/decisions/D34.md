# D34 — A bounded backend call's timeout floor is at least 300 seconds

## Context

Found by a real failure: a 120-second bound was too short for a multi-dependency `SpecBundle`.

## Decision

Any backend's own chosen timeout must be at least 300 seconds — a floor future backends/tuning may raise, never lower.
