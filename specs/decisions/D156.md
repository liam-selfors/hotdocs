# D156 — spec-completeness-assistant gains llm_backend, and every criterion fakes its dependencies explicitly

## Context

Second real attempt (after `D155`): `AC-ASSIST-001` now passes, but
the verdict reports `interface_drift`, and three other criteria fail.

The drift is the candidate adding `*, llm_backend=None` to `assist` —
a parameter `interface/library.md` never listed, though every other
model-calling component in this corpus (`generator`,
`provenance-tracker`, `component-gate-runner`) exposes exactly that
injection point. The candidate was right and the interface was
incomplete.

`AC-ASSIST-003` and `AC-ASSIST-007` failed because their fixtures, run
through the *real* `spec-linter`, were not lint-clean (`R-LINT-010`,
"no gherkin block"). Under `D155`'s own new short-circuit, a hard-fail
finding now empties every other field, so a lint-dirty fixture
silently breaks a criterion that has nothing to do with linting.
`AC-ASSIST-006`'s fixture never faked `generator`, the same omission
`AC-ASSIST-008` hit in round one.

## Decision

`interface/library.md` adds `llm_backend` and now lists, in order,
every dependency function `assist` calls, stating that a stand-in
must provide each one. `AC-ASSIST-002`/`003`/`006`/`007` are rewritten
so each fixture explicitly fakes `spec-linter` (no findings, or a
warn-only finding) plus every other dependency it reaches, decoupling
each criterion from the real linter's verdict on a synthetic fixture.
