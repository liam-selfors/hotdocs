# D284 — Verifier tests do not lint their fixtures

## Context

Seeds 9, 10 and 11 of `verifier-orchestrator` each failed one criterion
whose test asserted its fixture lints clean, each time on a different
rule. `R-AUTHOR-015` asks for that check only when the target checks a
precondition against `spec-linter`, and `verify` checks none.

## Decision

`interface/library.md` says `verify` checks no precondition against
`spec-linter`, so a test does not lint its fixture.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated.
