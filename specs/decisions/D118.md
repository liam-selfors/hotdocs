# D118 — spec-bundle-assembler lands at 14 of 15 after the transitive-type-resolution fix, one failure traced to a pre-existing graph-query-engine gap

## Context

`D114`–`D117` closed the chain of issues `R-ASSEMBLE-013` (transitive type resolution) and its own knock-on effects surfaced: the new `AC-ASSEMBLE-015` passed on the very first real retry; three regressions (unit files given frontmatter) were a single clean cause, closed by `D115`; a documentation gap in `spec-linter`'s own `interface/library.md` (`D116`) and the self-check instruction needing a literal worked call rather than corrected prose (`D117`) closed the rest.

The next real retry reached 14 of 15 criteria passing — up from the `D109` baseline of 11 of 14. The one remaining failure, `AC-ASSEMBLE-011`, is not a test-authoring or candidate defect: its self-check calls `spec-linter`'s `lint()` (correctly, per `D117`'s fix) against a fixture with zero decision files, which trips `graph-query-engine`'s own `_FRONTMATTER_ZERO_MATCH_GUARDS` substitution into a malformed SQL statement (`BinderException: Referenced column "id" not found`). Confirmed via `graph-query-engine`'s own hand-maintained suite: `test_ac_gqe_008_decision_lineage_has_last_ratified_commit` and `test_ac_gqe_009_decision_nodes_and_supersedes_edge` fail identically, and predate this entire session — a pre-existing, already-known gap in an already-stable, out-of-scope component, not a new regression.

## Decision

`spec-bundle-assembler` is accepted and swapped in at 14/15. `AC-ASSEMBLE-011`'s failure is recorded as a known limitation tracing to `graph-query-engine`'s own pre-existing decision-nodes gap, not chased here — fixing it is `graph-query-engine`'s own, separate, already-tracked work. `AC-ASSEMBLE-007`'s earlier known limitation (`D109`, `component_context`'s own documented verbatim return including a trailing newline) was not re-triggered this round and passes cleanly now; no longer a documented gap.
