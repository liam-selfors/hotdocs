# D259 — `R-LINT-004` and `R-LINT-018` findings name the components involved

## Context

Seed 1 of `spec-linter` reported a `depends_on` cycle once per member,
each finding naming only its own component, and an undeclared
guarantee dependency without naming the owner. `AC-LINT-004` and
`AC-LINT-018` expect those names in the finding; neither requirement
said so.

## Decision

A cycle's finding names every component on it, and `R-LINT-018`'s
undeclared-owner finding names the owner, each by `comp-<slug>` id.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated.
