# D69 — A generation prompt excludes the target's own historical narrative, not just a dependency's

## Context

The principle "a dependent needs the contract, not the internal rationale" was already correctly enforced for every dependency a generation prompt includes, but never applied to the target component's own `component.md` — which was included in full, every call. Measured directly: the Rationale/Open-questions/Change-log sections alone are 15-60% of a real component's body, depending on the component, explaining only why past decisions were made, never what the model must build.

## Decision

The bundle-assembly rule now excludes the target's own `Rationale`, `Open questions`, and `Change log` sections specifically — `Purpose`, `Scope`, `Definitions`, and the interface/data/acceptance/constraints pointer text stay, since (unlike a dependency) the target's own Scope and Definitions genuinely describe what to build.
