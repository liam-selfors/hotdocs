# D236 — `AC-ENDPOINT-005` and `008` give their provider fixtures exactly

## Context

Seed 10 of `llm-endpoint` failed two criteria, both on fixtures. A test
for `AC-ENDPOINT-005` wrote an openai-compatible `hotdocs.toml` without
its `[llm]` header, and one for `AC-ENDPOINT-008` assumed every HTTP
provider has a key variable, which Ollama does not.

## Decision

Both criteria give the `hotdocs.toml` table's exact values, and
`AC-ENDPOINT-008` names which providers take a key. That put
`llm-endpoint` over its word cap, so `R-ENDPOINT-002` loses two clauses of
explanation and `component.md`'s Rationale, history `D18` records, is cut
to one sentence.

## Consequences

`llm-endpoint` is regenerated.
