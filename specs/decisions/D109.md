# D109 — spec-bundle-assembler cold-generated for the first time, closing its temporary hand-authored exception

## Context

`spec-bundle-assembler` was one of two "temporary exception" hand-authored components (`cache-manager` being the other) that README.md always said nothing stopped a real `generator` from cold-regenerating later. `D100` required exactly that: adding `dependency_interfaces` (`R-ASSEMBLE-009`) to its own `CriterionBundle` meant the component could no longer just be hand-edited (`D36` forbids patching a rejected candidate, and by extension forbids hand-editing a component this corpus's own methodology says must be generated).

Across `D100`–`D108` (full round-by-round account in `verification/spec-bundle-assembler/BOOTSTRAP.md` and `verification/test-author/BOOTSTRAP.md`), reaching a clean bootstrap required: fixing `spec-bundle-assembler`'s own spec twice (`D100` additive field, `D101` a `PyYAML` misstep), and — the larger share of the work — fixing `test-author` seven times (`D102`–`D108`) for gaps in how it authors synthetic test fixtures and calls the real model, none of which were specific to `spec-bundle-assembler` itself; they surfaced here only because this was the first component with both real dependencies and corpus-wide lint-cleanliness preconditions to have `generated-test` criteria authored against it for real. A sandbox-contamination bug in the bootstrap driver itself (symlinked, not copied, dependency directories — a test's own synthetic fixture leaked into the real repo through the symlink) was also found and fixed (copy instead of symlink) during this arc.

The final real retry (`claude-opus-5-5`, with `R-ASSEMBLE-003`/`009`/`010`/`011` retry feedback) passed 11 of 14 criteria. Diagnosing the remaining 3 (never patched by hand, only inspected in the sandbox):

- `AC-ASSEMBLE-013` (3 of its sub-tests): synthetic fixtures leave trailing whitespace on lines, tripping `R-LINT-019` — the same class of "fixture isn't actually lint-clean" gap `R-AUTHOR-012`–`016` already closed several instances of, now with a new instance.
- `AC-ASSEMBLE-011`: two synthetic components (`bar`/`baz`) are non-sealed with no `realizes`, tripping `R-LINT-006` — `R-AUTHOR-013`'s sealed-by-default instruction is a SHOULD, not consistently followed by the model on every fixture.
- `AC-ASSEMBLE-010`: not a bug. `graph-query-engine`'s `component_context` view is documented as returning Purpose text "verbatim" (`interface/data/ComponentContext.md`), and its regex naturally includes a trailing newline; the AC's own Gherkin ("is exactly `Caches results.`") didn't anticipate that, so the candidate's `'Caches results.\n'` is arguably correct against the real dependency's own documented contract, not a defect in the candidate or in test-authoring.

## Decision

Per the standing practice of not chasing every last round indefinitely once failures stop recurring across rounds and start looking like normal last-mile imperfection: `spec-bundle-assembler`'s cold-generated candidate is accepted and swapped in at 11/14, `model_pin` set to `claude-opus-5-5`. The three gaps above are recorded as known limitations in `BOOTSTRAP.md`, not chased with a further round. The old hand-written `verification/spec-bundle-assembler/test_spec_bundle_assembler.py` (and its dedicated `conftest.py`) are retired — confirmed incompatible with the new candidate's own naming (`PreconditionFailureError` renamed `PreconditionFailure`) and fully superseded by the new per-criterion `generated-test` suite, including its one test with no direct `AC-id` (a sealed dependency's `source_path` resolution), which the new `AC-ASSEMBLE-011` fixture exercises too.
