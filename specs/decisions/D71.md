# D71 — Decision supersession: a real mechanism, not just a schema field

## Context

A field for marking one decision as replacing another had existed in the schema from the start, but had never once been used across the whole decision history — real superseding relationships existed only as unstructured prose.

## Decision

When a new decision's `supersedes` names a prior one, that prior record's `status` flips to `deprecated` as a metadata-only edit — the one sanctioned exception to decision-record immutability, since without it `deprecated` could never be set and `supersedes` would be a field with no consumer. `supersedes` is scoped to full replacement only; a partial reversal stays a plain prose cross-reference, never this formal field. A mechanical check enforces the relationship both directions: every `supersedes` must resolve to an actually-`deprecated` record, and every `deprecated` record must be named by some real `supersedes`.
