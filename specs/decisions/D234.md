# D234 — A criterion's kind is its `Kind:` line

## Context

Seed 10 of `spec-linter` failed every acceptance file in the corpus, 349
of them, on `R-LINT-010`: it looked for the kind somewhere other than the
`Kind: generated-test` line every file has. §5 said each criterion has
"a required `kind`" and never gave its syntax.

## Decision

§5 says the kind is the line `Kind: <kind>` after the `Covers:` tag, with
one of the three values, never a frontmatter field.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated.
