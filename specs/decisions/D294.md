# D294 — Re-ratify the secret allowlist after the Opus rebuild

## Context

After every component was regenerated, a full-repo secret scan flagged
fifteen findings that `.secret-allowlist.json`, sealed by `D196`, did
not cover: the planted keys in `AC-SECRET-008` and its regenerated
test, repeated-pattern fixture hashes in the new
provenance-checker and provenance-writer tests, a fake key in a
test-author test, and `verification/corpus-graph.json`, which since
`D286` carries the secret-scanner criteria's text. Every one is a
planted fixture. Because `component-gate-runner` scans the whole repo,
each component's manifest recorded `flagged`, and `hotdocs verify`
reported the tool not ready.

## Decision

The allowlist is the scanner's own `propose()` output: the 42 existing
entries plus 15, each `planted-fixture`, every flagged line checked by
hand. This record carries its hash.

## Consequences

A full scan is clean again. The 27 manifests written before this keep
the scan they actually ran, which was flagged; they are not rewritten by
hand, and each is re-recorded the next time its component is generated.
