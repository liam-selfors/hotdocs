# D298 — State the criterion-visibility rule, and guide authors by it

## Context

In the full regeneration of the tool's own components, most rejected
candidates traced to the spec, not the model. Two causes recurred. A
criterion expected behavior that no requirement stated, which the
generator, told by `D252` to implement nothing more than its bundle,
rightly left out. Or a criterion's test needed a fact its author could
not see, because the fact was written in another requirement, a decision
or a document outside the bundle: the step grammar, the lint rules, a
reply format, the sealed-fixture advice and the `supersedes` form were all
of this kind, and `D274` and `D284` are typical of the fixes. Users of the
tool face the same two traps with no one to diagnose them.

## Decision

`specs/architecture/criterion-visibility.md` states what the generator, a
criterion's test author and a judge each see, and the two rules that
follow: every behavior a criterion checks is stated in a requirement or
the interface, and every fact a criterion's test needs is in that
criterion, its covered requirement or the interface. No component
implements it yet; `spec-reviewer` will check specs against it.

`AUTHORING.md`, at the repository root and outside every bundle, is a
practical guide for users built on the same rules, with examples from the
regeneration.

## Consequences

No bundle hash changes. Later components cite this record when they
check or enforce the rule.
