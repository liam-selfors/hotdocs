# D269 — `test-author`'s interface states the reply markers

## Context

Seed 1 of `test-author` failed eight criteria whose mock models replied
with bare fenced code. The marker format is in `R-AUTHOR-009`, but a
criterion's test is written from that criterion, the requirement it
covers and `interface/`, so authors of every other criterion never saw
it.

## Decision

`interface/library.md` gives the marker lines and pins the nonce to
`secrets.token_hex(16)`, read from the prompt. The change log points to
the decisions instead, keeping the component under its word cap.

## Consequences

`test-author` is regenerated.
