# D80 — Mutation-score thresholds for generated-test criteria

## Context

`SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §6 states a `generated-test` criterion's mutation score must meet 80% (general) or 90% (freeform-derived) or the merge fails, with no decision record backing either number — a real choice, since a weaker or stronger bar was equally available and changes how much a passing test actually proves.

## Decision

80% is the general-case mutation-score floor for a `generated-test` criterion; 90% applies to a freeform-derived one — a stricter bar for the case with less structure to lean on for trustworthiness. Falling short of the applicable floor fails the merge, the same severity as any other `generated-test` verification failure. (This record captures the two numbers and that a freeform-derived criterion is held to the higher one; it does not attempt to pin down a precise definition of "freeform-derived" beyond what `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` §5's `kind` table already gives, since no other spec in this corpus elaborates the term further — a gap worth closing in its own right if the distinction ever needs to be operationalized by `verifier-orchestrator` rather than just stated.)
