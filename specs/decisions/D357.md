# D357 — Re-ratify the secret allowlist after the D353-D356 regeneration

## Context

The regeneration for `D353`-`D356` re-authored most generated tests. Three
new ones hold planted fixtures, fixed hex runs the scanner reads as
high-entropy strings, that the allowlist sealed by `D351` did not cover:
`verification/provenance-checker/generated-test/test_ac_provcheck_001.py`,
`verification/provenance-checker/generated-test/test_ac_provcheck_004.py`
and `verification/provenance-writer/generated-test/test_ac_prov_020.py`.
Re-recording would have recorded every manifest as flagged.

## Decision

The allowlist is the scanner's own `propose()` output, checked by hand:
the 40 entries sealed by `D351` plus those three, each `planted-fixture`.
One older entry for `test_ac_prov_020.py` no longer matches; it is kept,
since an unused entry never blocks. This record carries the file's hash.

## Consequences

A full scan is clean, and the re-record of every component proceeds with
clean scans.
