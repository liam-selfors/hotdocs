# D382 — The bundle hash covers only the component text a bundle reads

## Context

`R-ASSEMBLE-007` hashed every input file's raw bytes. The bundle leaves
out a target's `## Rationale`, `## Open questions` and `## Change log`
(`D69`) and takes only `## Purpose` from a dependency's `component.md`
(`D21`), yet an edit anywhere in those files changed the hash: the
component regenerated after a change-log edit, and every dependent
regenerated after an edit to a dependency's `Scope`. Trimming history
out of specs, which the spec review of 2026-11-26 recommends, would
itself force those regenerations.

## Decision

A `component.md` contributes to `spec_bundle_hash` only what the bundle
reads: a target's without its three excluded sections, and a
dependency's without every section but `## Purpose`; frontmatter and
the title stay. Every other input is hashed as before.

## Consequences

Editing a component's history sections, or any section of a dependency
but its `Purpose`, forces no regeneration.

Every `spec_bundle_hash` changes once, so every manifest misses and the
whole corpus regenerates in the wave that lands this.
