# D172 — The mutation operators' exact swaps are pinned

## Context

Regenerating `verifier-orchestrator` for `D171` produced a gate that
passed all of its hand tests yet enumerated fewer mutation sites than
the one it replaced: 31 against 41 on the same module. `R-VERIFY-006`
pinned the four operator classes (`D159`) but not their swap tables, and
the new candidate chose a different comparison pairing (`<`↔`>` rather
than `<`↔`>=`), dropped `is`/`in`, and dropped floor-division and
modulo. Scores on landed components moved by a few points
(0.878 to 0.839 on one) with the verdicts unchanged. That is a
silent recalibration of the bar that every component's landing depends
on, caused by an unrelated regeneration.

## Decision

`R-VERIFY-006` states the exact swaps, copied from the gate the corpus
was calibrated against, and says a regeneration must not change them.
The candidate is regenerated against the pinned text. Nothing about the
bar itself changes.
