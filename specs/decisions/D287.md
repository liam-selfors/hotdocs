# D287 — A freeform-derived criterion is one with no Given/When/Then block

## Context

`D80` set the mutation bar at 80% in general and 90% for a
freeform-derived criterion, and left "freeform-derived" undefined until
`verifier-orchestrator` had to act on it. The regenerated one read every
`generated-test` criterion as freeform-derived and applied 90%, failing
`component-gate-runner` at 0.85 with every criterion passing; the
previous verifier applied 80% throughout.

## Decision

`R-VERIFY-006`: the bar is 0.8, or 0.9 when a `generated-test` criterion
is written with no Given/When/Then block. `R-LINT-010` requires that
block of every criterion, so the corpus is held to 0.8, as every
component verified so far was.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated.
