# D117 — R-AUTHOR-015's self-check needs a literal worked call, not just a corrected signature in prose

## Context

With `D116`'s documentation fix landed, the next `spec-bundle-assembler` retry showed real but incomplete improvement: two previously-failing criteria (`AC-ASSEMBLE-005`/`010`) now call `lint()` correctly, but three others (`AC-ASSEMBLE-002`/`003`/`015`) still call it as `lint(repo_root)` — a single positional argument, binding `repo_root`'s value to `paths` and leaving the real `repo_root` parameter unfilled. The corrected signature in prose (`D116`) was evidently not reliably sufficient on its own, the identical lesson `R-AUTHOR-010`/`011` already learned for their own conventions.

## Decision

`R-AUTHOR-015` is amended: the self-check instruction MUST include the exact call as a literal worked snippet — `lint(None, repo_root)` — not prose describing the signature alone. `test-author` is cold-regenerated against this amendment; `spec-bundle-assembler` is retried once more.
