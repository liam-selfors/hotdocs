# D240 — `AC-ENDPOINT-012` covers joining the `claude` command's message stream

## Context

Seed 13 of `llm-endpoint` passed every criterion at a mutation score of
0.75. Most surviving mutants were in the stream-joining code `D225`
added: no criterion exercised it.

## Decision

`AC-ENDPOINT-012` feeds the backend a recorded stream with two
`assistant` messages, a thinking block, and a `result`, then an error
result, then plain text, and checks each outcome and the flags passed. To stay under the word cap,
`R-ENDPOINT-003` keeps only a clause of why each flag is passed; `D35`
and `D82` hold the rest.

Seed 16 then failed only on the plain-text case: the criterion expected
`"hello"` where the recorder printed `"hello\n"`, and the requirement
says the reply is the output as printed. The criterion now gives the
exact stdout.

## Consequences

`llm-endpoint` is regenerated.
