# D380 — Size caps measure what each model reads

## Context

`component_doc_words` capped a component at 4,000 words of its own
files: `component.md`, `requirements/`, `interface/`, `constraints.md`
and `acceptance/` (`D79`). The spec review of 2026-11-26 measured what
that counts against what the models read:

- 38% of the counted words (46,338 of 120,671) are acceptance criteria,
  which the generator never sees and each test author sees one at a
  time.
- Documents a component's `Implements:` line appends reach its
  generator and every test author uncounted: 79,805 words across the
  corpus. `verifier-orchestrator` sat at 3,997 counted words while its
  generator read about 14,900.
- Dependency interfaces, also in every bundle, are not counted either.

A component near the cap was trimmed or split to satisfy a number that
did not track how much its models were asked to hold.

## Decision

1. `component_doc_words` is replaced by two dimensions, each from
   `graph-query-engine`'s `bundle_size` view (`R-GQE-009`):
   `generator_words`, what the component's generator reads, and
   `criterion_words_max`, the largest single criterion's test-author
   bundle. The glossary, `vars` and appended type files, the same for
   most bundles, are left out.
2. Thresholds, set from the corpus's distribution after `D381` (median
   generator bundle about 3,800 words, largest about 12,000): warn above
   8,000 and fail above 14,000 for `generator_words`; warn above 6,000
   and fail above 12,000 for `criterion_words_max`. Like `D17`'s, they
   are a first pass, to be revisited.
3. The acceptance-count cap, the per-file caps and the other dimensions
   are unchanged (`specs/architecture/size-caps.md`).

## Consequences

A component's own criteria no longer crowd out its requirements, and a
heavy `Implements:` document now counts where it lands. Splitting a
component is prompted by what its generator actually holds.
`component_size` stays as `corpus-graph-builder`'s display figure.

Regenerated: `graph-query-engine` (the view), `size-checker`, and the
components their code hashes reach.
