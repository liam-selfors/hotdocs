# D27 — Compiled and generated-test failures carry structured failure detail

## Decision

`VerificationReport.criteria[].failure_detail` (`{message, expected, actual}`) is populated for any failing `compiled`/`generated-test` criterion whose underlying check is a direct equality/containment match — mirroring `llm-judge`'s `rationale` field for the other `kind`. Never threaded into `requirement_results` or `retry_feedback` — both have their own content boundary this does not reopen.
