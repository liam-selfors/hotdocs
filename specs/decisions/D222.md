# D222 — `R-LINT-027` joins wrapped paths and skips placeholders; `AC-COMPILE-012` checks the call line

## Context

The first complete generation of `spec-linter` from its spec (seed 2)
found two hard failures in the real corpus that the hand-written linter
did not: a section pointer whose path is hard-wrapped inside its
backticks, and `R-LINT-027`'s own text quoting the pattern with
`<file>`. The hand-written version removed whitespace inside the
backticks and skipped `<...>` placeholders, as `R-LINT-028` already
spells out for glossary citations (`D176`), but `R-LINT-027` never said
so. Everything else the candidate reported over the whole corpus matched.

## Decision

`R-LINT-027` states both rules. Its closing sentence of rationale goes,
to keep `spec-linter` under its word cap.

Separately, seed 10 of `criterion-compiler` failed only `AC-COMPILE-012`,
whose test required the name `user_id` to appear nowhere in the emitted
source, though the grammar binds it as a local in the `Given` line. The
criterion now checks the call line.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated against it.
