# D51 — VAR-LICENSE-CORPUS-PATH names license-scanner's real reference-corpus location

## Context

A real, otherwise well-reasoned candidate defaulted its reference-corpus lookup to `.hotdocs/` — gitignored, local-only, and forbidden for anything a gate's pass/fail decision depends on. Nothing in the component's own spec named a real location for this input at all.

## Decision

New var declared; the requirement now states explicitly where the real corpus lives, and that a missing or unreachable corpus must still produce a real, recorded clean result — never an error, never a silent skip.
