# D281 — `VerificationReport`'s prose shortened to fit the word cap

## Context

`D280`'s additions took `verifier-orchestrator` to 4,039 words, over the
4,000-word cap.

## Decision

Three explanatory passages in `interface/data/VerificationReport.md`
are shortened, the `result` vocabulary note, the `test_authoring`
rationale and the `skipped`-counts-as-`fail` explanation, with no rule
changed.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated from the shortened text.
