# D39 — generate() caches only non-empty, successful results

## Context

`generate()` was writing every non-`model_call_error` response to cache unconditionally, including responses that parsed to zero files — violating `GenerationResult`'s own "exactly one of files/model_call_error/refused populated" contract and silently poisoning the cache.

## Decision

`cache_manager.put()` only runs when parsing returned at least one file.
