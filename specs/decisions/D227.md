# D227 — `AC-LINT-028` stops citing a missing glossary entry, and `R-LINT-027` deletes wrap whitespace

## Context

Seed 5 of `spec-linter` reported two hard failures in the real corpus.
One was correct: `AC-LINT-028` described its fixture by writing a real
citation of a glossary entry that does not exist, wrapped between
"glossary's" and the quote. `R-LINT-028` reads wrapped citations, so the
citation should always have failed; the hand-written linter's pattern
needed a single literal space there and missed it. The other was the
candidate collapsing a wrapped path's whitespace to one space where
`R-LINT-027` meant deleting it, which "removed" left open.

## Decision

`AC-LINT-028`'s first scenario describes its fixture's entries without
writing a citation. `R-LINT-027` says the whitespace is deleted, not
collapsed.

## Consequences

The corpus no longer holds a citation the linter should reject, and the
regenerated linter is stricter than the hand-written one in this one
respect.
