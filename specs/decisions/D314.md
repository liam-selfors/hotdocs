# D314 — `hotdocs generate` runs the attempt loop, and counts its attempts from the right directory

## Context

`component-gate-runner` generated once and verified once. With
`generation-loop` the attempts, their feedback and the closures move
there. Separately, the manifest's `attempts` count read
`.hotdocs/attempts/<slug>/` while `provenance-tracker` writes attempt
records to `.hotdocs/attempts/<component_id>/`, so every manifest in the
corpus recorded `0` attempts.

## Decision

On a miss, the gate runner assembles the bundle and calls
`run_attempts` once, writing one attempt record per attempt through its
callback, and passes the loop's attempt list to `provenance-writer` as
`attempt_history`. `attempts` is counted in
`.hotdocs/attempts/<component_id>/`. A blocked result's `block_reason`
gains `surface_check_failed` and `refused`. `hotdocs generate` gains
`--max-attempts` (default `3`; `1` is the old one-shot behavior),
`--mode=single|agentic` and `--allow-unisolated-exec`; the first two
default from a `[generate]` table in `hotdocs.toml`. The CLI follows
`specs/architecture/cli-conventions.md`.

## Consequences

The gate runner no longer depends on `generator`, `test-author` or
`llm-endpoint` directly.
