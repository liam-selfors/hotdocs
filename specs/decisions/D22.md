# D22 — Component Purpose is dependent-safe, length-capped, and separate from the contract

## Context

A generating model needs to know a dependency's intent, not just its interface signature, to avoid a call that's type-correct but wrong per intent. Purpose text must stay free of implementation rationale so it's safe to share with dependents.

## Decision

`Purpose` is a short (≤150-word hard cap), dependent-safe description of what a component does and why it exists — no implementation rationale, no decision citations. It reaches dependents through `graph-query-engine`'s `component_context` view and `spec-bundle-assembler`'s `dependency_context` field — a field distinct from `dependency_interfaces`, which stays exactly the contract.
