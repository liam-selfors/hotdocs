# D283 — Verifier fixture criteria always have Given, When and Then

## Context

Seed 10 of `verifier-orchestrator` failed only `AC-VERIFY-014`, whose
fixture criterion again had no Given step, though `D282` put
`SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` in the bundle: the guidelines never state that
every criterion needs all three, which is `R-LINT-010`'s rule, and the
step grammar treats Given steps as setup.

## Decision

`interface/library.md`'s fixture notes say every fixture criterion has
at least one Given, one When and one Then step. `SPEC_GUIDELINES.md` is
not edited, since that would change every bundle that includes it.

## Consequences

`verifier-orchestrator` is regenerated.
