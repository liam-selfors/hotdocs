# D237 — `Corpus` paths are absolute

## Context

Seed 12 of `spec-linter` reported exactly the hand-written linter's
findings over the whole corpus, but its `load_corpus` returned glossary
paths relative to `repo_root`, and `spec-bundle-assembler`, opening one,
failed from a different working directory. `Corpus.md` (`D208`) never
said whether `Doc.path` and `glossary_files` are absolute.

## Decision

`Corpus.md` says `path` and `glossary_files` entries are absolute, and
`rel_path` relative to `repo_root`, in no more words than before.

## Consequences

`spec-linter` is regenerated.
