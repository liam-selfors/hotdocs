# R-MUT-002

**R-MUT-002** — The mutants MUST be exactly `mutation-runs.md`'s sites,
  in its order: the `python` adapter's `strength_sites` over the copy,
  then each declared asset's in declaration order, each applied alone
  with its adapter's `apply_site` and the copy restored after it. A
  mutant is killed when its run exits nonzero or times out; the score
  is killed over mutants, one score for the component, never an average
  (`D124`). The sites were calibrated against the bar, so a
  regeneration MUST NOT change them.
