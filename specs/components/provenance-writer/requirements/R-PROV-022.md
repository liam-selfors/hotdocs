# R-PROV-022

**R-PROV-022** — The manifest's `assets` and `drivers` MUST each be the
  `verification_report`'s field of that name, copied verbatim, entries
  and order unchanged; `[]` when `verification_report` is `null` or has
  no such field or it is `null`. Never computed here (`D343`, `D373`).
